How long do Westchester towns take?

See how long your town took to close requests like yours, and read the requests behind the number.

Five Westchester towns, Mount Pleasant, Greenburgh, Mount Vernon, North Castle and Peekskill, take residents' requests through SeeClickFix: potholes, fallen trees, blocked storm drains, broken streetlights and more. SeeClickFix publishes when each request was opened and closed. Pick a kind of request and a town to see how long it usually took, how that compares with the other towns, and the requests themselves. Yonkers and most other towns don't publish their records, so they aren't here.
